# Development Pipeline Design

## Use this skill when
Use this skill after research is complete and approved, and the next step is to freeze the intended solution before planning or implementation.

## Workflow
1. Load `../development-pipeline/references/design.md`.
2. Produce the gated `discussion.md` first and pause for human approval.
3. After approval, complete the remaining design artifacts, including `structure-outline.md`.
4. Stop when the design is approved and ready to hand off to planning.

## Boundaries
- Do not skip the discussion gate.
- Do not write implementation code in this phase.
- Keep the design consistent with confirmed research findings.
